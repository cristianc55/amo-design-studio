Santa Croce library

Architecture / Spatial proposal · Turin
View all imagesOriginal post

A library within the Church of Santa Croce, Turin. A contemporary structure follows the geometry of the existing nave, creating a vertical route for reading, observation and contemplation. Platforms, inhabited steps and seating areas rise above a film projection room and reconnect with the choir balcony. The intervention preserves the memory of the historic space through a clear, contemporary and reversible addition.
